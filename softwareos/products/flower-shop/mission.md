# Product Mission

> Customer: [../../customer/overview.md](../../customer/overview.md)

**Brand:** Flower by Event — *"Choose the occasion, we choose the flowers."*

## Problem

People buying flowers for personal occasions and special events often feel lost or overwhelmed. They don't know which flowers suit the occasion, what their budget can buy, or how to put together a beautiful arrangement — and they're afraid of getting it wrong.

## Target Users

Anyone buying flowers for a personal occasion without flower knowledge — birthdays, romance and anniversaries, life events (new baby, sympathy, congratulations), and thank-yous. Ages roughly 20–45 is a loose hint, not a definition.

Customers who already know flowers are served too, through traditional browsing and build-your-own.

## Solution

An online flower shop that makes customers feel supported rather than lost: every option is available, but they never have to face them all at once. Three ways to shop:

1. **Guided questionnaire** — occasion → budget → color palette → a recommended ready-made bouquet, or a starting mix of individual flowers within budget.
2. **Traditional browsing** — a catalog of ready-made bouquets with filters for those who know what they want.
3. **Build your own** — compose a bouquet stem by stem from a curated set of flowers.

**Differentiator:** the guided questionnaire, encoding occasion "flower etiquette" and budget-to-size guidance, so no flower knowledge is needed.
