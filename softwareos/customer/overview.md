# Flower Shop — Overview

## Business

Flower Shop (brand: **Flower by Event**) is a new, online-first flower business in Israel. There is no physical store: bouquets are made in-house from a small local workspace and delivered within one local delivery area (exact area TBD). The website is in English; prices are in Israeli shekels (ILS).

## Goals & Engagement

Make buying flowers for personal occasions and special events simple, accessible, and personalized — especially for people who don't know how to choose flowers, work within a budget, or put together a good arrangement.

Engagement: we build and run the web store and API ourselves (own business, founder-run).

- **Repeat customers** — share of buyers who order again; target TBD.

## Known Risks & Open Questions

- **Language:** the site is English-only for simplicity, but the local market is mostly Hebrew-speaking. Hebrew (RTL) support is a likely early follow-up — build i18n-ready from the start.
- **Capacity:** the founder covers every role for now. Florist and delivery capacity limit how many orders can be fulfilled per day.
- **Fulfillment:** delivery area, delivery method (own driver vs. courier service), and daily order capacity are not yet defined.
